AGENCY FOUNDATION / 02
Founders
& governance
Ownership, authority and departure principles.
Founders: Issam Ben Hamad and Mohamed Bettaieb
Working draft for founder review. Recorded rules reflect the discussion; items marked OPEN are not yet
decided. Agency name remains open.
01 / Ownership and shared responsibilities
Issam Ben Hamad: Founder; 50% ownership and equal voting rights.
Mohamed Bettaieb: Founder; 50% ownership and equal voting rights.
Both founders jointly manage agency decisions, roles, members and finances. Either founder
may act as project manager or developer according to project needs.
02 / Decision-making
Routine decisions: The relevant specialist has authority within an agreed scope and
budget.
Major agency decisions: Both founders' agreement is required in principle. A random pick
is the agreed fallback for unresolved deadlocks.
OPEN: Whether the random fallback applies to every major decision or whether some
matters always require mutual consent. The draw method, timing, and how to resolve
disputed or equal expertise are also open.
03 / Founder departure
If Issam leaves: Full ownership goes to Mohamed Bettaieb.
If Mohamed leaves: Full ownership goes to Issam Ben Hamad.
These statements record the founders' intended departure arrangement; this draft does not
execute an ownership transfer.
OPEN: Compensation or buyout, notice, transfer procedure, ongoing projects, unpaid
earnings, liabilities and handover.
04 / OPEN - governance details
Definition of major decisions; spending limits; areas of specialization; decision records; and
the procedure for changing these rules.
Project PM authority operates within the agency's agreed scope and budget. Ownership
percentages are separate from project compensation.

AGENCY FOUNDATION / 03
Services
& project delivery
Staffing, project authority and release workflow.
Founders: Issam Ben Hamad and Mohamed Bettaieb
Working draft for founder review. Recorded rules reflect the discussion; items marked OPEN are not yet
decided. Agency name remains open.
01 / Minimum project staffing
Every project requires at least two people: a project manager (PM) and at least one
developer. Either founder may take either role depending on the project. Additional
developers may be recruited per project when needed.
02 / Project manager responsibilities
The PM manages the work, deadlines, meetings, validation of each phase, client
communication and project decisions. Routine authority remains within the agreed scope and
budget.
Developer role: Performs project development. Detailed responsibilities, testing ownership,
documentation and reporting expectations remain OPEN.
03 / Pricing and external developers
The founders are responsible for the pricing approach at this stage. This responsibility may
later be delegated to a CTO or team lead; no delegation is currently defined.
External developers receive a fixed price decided by a founder or the PM. Financial allocation
rules and unresolved calculation details are recorded in Document 04.
04 / Delivery workflow
Building > Testing > PM validation > Delivery
PM validation precedes delivery, and the PM is responsible for validating each project phase.
05 / OPEN - delivery procedures
Service packages; estimates and proposals; scope and milestones; acceptance criteria;
change requests; testing standards and responsibility; handover; client acceptance;
maintenance and support.
No warranty period, support commitment or detailed developer checklist has been agreed in
this draft.

===== 04_Finance_and_Administration_Draft.pdf =====
FOUNDING WORKSHOP  |  Draft v0.1  |  02 October 2026
1
AGENCY FOUNDATION / 04
Finance
& administration
Project allocations, expenses and payment methods.
Founders: Issam Ben Hamad and Mohamed Bettaieb
Working draft for founder review. Recorded rules reflect the discussion; items marked OPEN are not yet
decided. Agency name remains open.
01 / Expenses and payments
Identified expenses: AI model subscriptions, infrastructure, Upwork Connects and Upwork
subscriptions.
Payments: Through Upwork for Upwork clients; cash for clients outside Upwork for now. No
separate payment system is currently defined.
02 / Recorded allocation principles
The agency retains 10% per project. Fees, if any, are deducted. External developers receive
an agreed fixed payment before the remaining amount is distributed.
The remaining distributable project amount is allocated 60% to the developer and 40% to
the PM. Ownership remains 50% per founder and is separate from this role-based project
allocation.
Founders' stated outline: Project money - fees, if any - 10% for the agency = net amount
to divide between workers. External developer fixed payments must also be accounted for
before the 60/40 split.
03 / OPEN - calculation details
The base for the agency's 10% and the deduction order relative to external developer
payments remain unresolved. No numeric example or final calculation formula is adopted
here.
Allocation of the 60% share when all development is performed by fixed-price external
developers; treatment of shared subscriptions and other costs; tax treatment; refunds and
project losses; and payment timing remain open.
04 / OPEN - administration and compensation
Founder financial or equipment contributions; detailed compensation arrangements;
administrative responsibilities; cash records and invoicing; control and use of the agency
reserve.
The minimum PM-and-developer requirement is project staffing, not a confirmed financial
contribution by either founder.
